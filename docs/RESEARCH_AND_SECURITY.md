# SecureCAI: research and security position

## Purpose

SecureCAI is a human-in-the-loop demonstrator for high-stakes interpreting support. It makes terminology, uncertainty, risk, and human decisions visible.

## Design commitments

1. The interpreter retains decision authority.
2. The system separates suggestion from acceptance.
3. Risk flags require attention but do not determine meaning.
4. Synthetic or approved non-sensitive examples support demonstrations.
5. Data minimisation and local processing should precede any institutional deployment.
6. Every future AI component requires evaluation against human-reviewed material.

## Proposed evaluation

- terminology precision and recall;
- false-positive and false-negative rates for risk flags;
- expert agreement on severity;
- acceptance, editing, rejection, and escalation rates;
- time spent per segment;
- perceived cognitive load;
- trust calibration;
- error detectability;
- reproducibility across English–Arabic domains.

## Governance questions

Before real deployment, an institution should define:

- information classifications permitted in the system;
- approved models and hosting arrangements;
- retention and deletion rules;
- access controls and audit procedures;
- responsibility for errors;
- incident reporting;
- human override and tool-failure procedures.

This repository contains no operational or classified material.
