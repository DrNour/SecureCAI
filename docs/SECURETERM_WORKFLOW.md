# SecureTerm Pro: professional workflow

SecureTerm Pro is designed around a practical professional problem: interpreters and translators often receive briefing material that contains inconsistent terminology, unfamiliar entities, numbers, acronyms, and context-dependent expressions.

The application helps create a validated assignment termbase and preparation sheet.

## Suggested workshop demonstration

1. Load the synthetic briefing.
2. Inspect extracted numbers, entities, and terminology.
3. Add an Arabic equivalent or change a status to Needs verification.
4. Read the preparation questions.
5. Download the termbase and briefing sheet.
6. Discuss which decisions require an expert, institutional source, or policy owner.

## Professional boundary

AI-assisted extraction is useful for finding candidates. It does not establish the official equivalent. The interpreter, translator, terminology manager, or institution must validate the termbase.

## Research potential

The tool supports studies of terminology consistency, briefing quality, preparation time, expert disagreement, unresolved terminology, and the relationship between AI assistance and professional judgement.
